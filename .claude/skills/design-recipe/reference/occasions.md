# Recipes by occasion

The brand is one voice with many tones. These recipes show how the ingredients flex across the occasions we meet most. Each gives the arc position, a suggested lead direction, archetype, hero, how the dials turn, the moves, a line of copy and the trap. They are starting points: the direction engine and the brief still decide.

| Occasion | Arc | Lead direction | Archetype | Hero | Dials | Moves | Words | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch / campaign hero** | Anticipation (loudest) | Cinematic | Stage | The idea as headline, or the one number that proves it | Loud +2, Premium +1 | Tight display + wide caption; white action; one grid break | "Every match, official." | Listing features; they belong one scroll down |
| **Tournament announcement** | Anticipation | Broadcast (Cinematic if L2) | Stage + scoreboard band | Date/time or prize - ask which the audience cares about | Local up | Caption over hero number; scoreboard grid for format, slots, fee | "Karachi Valorant Open. 32 teams. Saturday, 8:00 PM PKT." | "Limited slots!" when they aren't |
| **Match-day countdown, push** | Nerves | Broadcast | Face-off / single-line stage | Time left + the one action | Technical up, Playful down | Versus lockup; cue on the time | "Check-in closes in 15 minutes. Night Owls, you're not in yet." | Hype language for a nervous captain |
| **The live moment** | Match | Broadcast / Console | Pulse or face-off with live state | Current state (map, score, round) | Quiet all the way down | One live dot; mono timestamps; grid | "Map 3 · Night Owls lead 9–6" | Overlays over the play; sponsors animating mid-round |
| **Victory / results** | Outcome | Trophy | Verdict, then spotlight on MVP | Winner's name | Loud +1 for the champion only; Human up | Resolved versus lockup; cue underline; slow push-in | "Night Owls are champions. 3–1 over Crimson Five in a final that went the distance." | Mocking the runner-up; red; confetti |
| **Welcome / first run** | Anticipation (new person) | Community | Fork, then path | The first useful action | Human up, Technical down, Premium −1 | Cut-edge choice cards; path with only step one lit | "Join a cup this weekend, or host your own. You can do both later." | A tour of everything |
| **Sponsor placement** | Any | Co-brand | Unchanged; partner sits inside | Never the sponsor - the event stays hero | Premium up | Mono "presented by" caption; partner mark in a neutral well | "KARACHI VALORANT OPEN · PRESENTED BY [PARTNER]" | Logos in every corner; partner colours taking the stage |
| **Venue screens** | All, over hours | Broadcast, scaled | Pulse between, face-off before, verdict after | Next match + time, readable at 10 m | Quiet up; scale up | Enormous hero number; scoreboard schedule | "Next on Station 4 · 20:30 · Zero Ping vs Delta Squad" | Constant motion that becomes invisible within an hour |
| **Money: payouts, receipts, fees** | Outcome / trouble | Daylight | Scoreboard / verdict | The amount and its date | Serious up, Technical up | Ink tables; exact figures; one cue on status | "PKR 50,000 paid to Night Owls on 18 Nov, 14:20 PKT." | Rounding, vague timing, celebratory tone on financial documents |
| **Bad news** | Trouble | The surface's own, stripped | Stage, stripped: statement, reason, next step | The new fact | Quiet fully down, Serious fully up | Type only; cue only on the new time | "The final now starts at 9:00 PM, one hour late, because of a server outage. Check-in stays open until 8:45 PM." | Euphemism, blame, playfulness |
| **Empty / waiting states** | Before anything | The surface's own | Stage, small | Why it's empty + the one action | Quiet down, Human up | Type only; plenty of black; perhaps a faint grid | "No matches yet. The bracket appears here when check-in closes." | Sad mascots; truly blank screens that look broken |
| **Editorial recap / story** | Belonging | Editorial | Stage opener, spotlight, inline scoreboard | The moment that defined the event | Human up | Pull quotes; datelines as captions; documentary photos | "The four maps that made Night Owls champions." | Chopping a story into dashboard cards |
| **Themed / charity / league event** | Any | Themed event | By surface | From the event's own truth | Per event | A sub-brand world framed by our invariants | From the event's subject | Theme borrowed from a trend, not the event |

## When the brief asks for something else entirely

A charity cup with a children's hospital, a women's league with its own identity, a publisher partnership with strict co-branding, a retro night. The recipe still applies (moment, truth, idea, hero); the direction changes. Keep the invariants (honest words, one hero, cue-light discipline, respect for players) and let the variables (palette temperature, typeface, texture, motion energy, shape) move. **Ask what the occasion needs before assuming it needs our default at full volume.**
