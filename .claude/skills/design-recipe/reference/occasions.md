# Recipes by occasion

The brand is one voice with many tones. A chef caters a wedding and a wake with the same kitchen and very different menus. These recipes show how the ingredients flex across the occasions Esportra meets most. Each is a **starting point** - the direction engine and the brief still decide - and each ends with the failure we see most often and its fix.

Format for every occasion: arc position · lead direction · archetype · hero · dial turns · moves · words · formats · failure → fix.

---

## 1. Launch or campaign hero

- **Arc:** anticipation, at its loudest. **Lead:** Cinematic (+ Broadcast facts band). **Archetype:** Stage, then scoreboard / spotlight / fork bands below.
- **Hero:** the single-minded idea as a headline, or the one number that proves it.
- **Dials:** Loud +2, Premium +1. Still no exclamation marks.
- **Moves:** tight display + wide caption at maximum contrast; white speaks, rose confirms; one grid break.
- **Words:** "Every match, official." · "Run your cup like a final."
- **Formats:** 16:9 hero + bands; 4:5 feed (hero + action); 9:16 story (hero word + action only); Discord banner (hero word + caption).
- **Failure → fix:** *Three value propositions crammed into the hero* → move features to band two; the hero carries one idea.

## 2. Tournament announcement

- **Arc:** anticipation. **Lead:** Broadcast (Cinematic when L2 and the event is major). **Archetype:** Stage + scoreboard band.
- **Hero:** date/time **or** prize pool - ask which this audience cares about more (grassroots: often the date and the city; established: often the prize).
- **Dials:** Local up (city, currency, time zone).
- **Moves:** caption over hero number; scoreboard grid for format, slots, fee, check-in time.
- **Words:** "Karachi Valorant Open. 32 teams. Saturday, 8:00 PM PKT." · Action: "Enter your team".
- **Formats:** 4:5 feed, 9:16 story, Discord embed, tournament page header, venue poster (Daylight).
- **Failure → fix:** *"Limited slots!" when 30 of 32 are open* → show the real count only when it becomes meaningful ("4 slots left").

## 3. Match-day countdown and notifications

- **Arc:** nerves. **Lead:** Broadcast. **Archetype:** single-line stage (push), face-off (in-app).
- **Hero:** the time left and the one action.
- **Dials:** Technical up, Playful down.
- **Moves:** versus lockup (in-app); cue under the time.
- **Words:** Push T-30: "Check-in is open. 30 minutes to check in Night Owls." T-10: "10 minutes left to check in Night Owls." After: "You're in. First match 8:30 PM, Station 4."
- **Failure → fix:** *Hype ("Get ready to dominate!")* → instructions; a nervous captain wants the next step, not adrenaline.

## 4. The live moment

- **Arc:** match. **Lead:** Broadcast (Console for staff operations views). **Archetype:** Pulse or face-off with a live state.
- **Hero:** the current state (map, score, round).
- **Dials:** Quiet all the way down. The game is the show.
- **Moves:** one live dot (the cue), mono timestamps, scoreboard grid.
- **Words:** "Map 3 · Night Owls lead 9–6".
- **Failure → fix:** *Sponsor banners animating during rounds, overlays covering play* → sponsors between maps only; overlays at the frame edges; nothing moves except the live values.

## 5. Victory and results

- **Arc:** outcome. **Lead:** Trophy (for finals) / Broadcast (for everyday results). **Archetype:** Verdict, then a Spotlight on the MVP.
- **Hero:** the winner's name.
- **Dials:** Loud +1 for the champion only; Human up.
- **Moves:** resolved versus lockup (winner white, runners-up grey); cue underline; slow push-in on the photo.
- **Words:** "Night Owls are champions. 3–1 over Crimson Five in a final that went the distance."
- **Formats:** 4:5 and 9:16 share cards; venue screen reveal; in-app bracket final node.
- **Failure → fix:** *Red for the loser, confetti, "rekt"* → grey and respect; one reveal and a long hold.

## 6. Welcome and first run

- **Arc:** anticipation (for someone new). **Lead:** Community. **Archetype:** Fork, then Path.
- **Hero:** the first useful action.
- **Dials:** Human up, Technical down, Premium −1.
- **Moves:** cut-edge choice cards; the path with only step one lit.
- **Words:** "Join a cup this weekend, or host your own. You can do both later."
- **Failure → fix:** *A five-screen product tour* → one decision, then learn by doing; contextual hints later.

## 7. Sponsor and partner placement

- **Arc:** any. **Lead:** Co-brand (decide the relationship first). **Archetype:** unchanged; the partner sits inside it.
- **Hero:** never the sponsor unless the partner leads.
- **Dials:** Premium up.
- **Moves:** mono "presented by" caption; partner mark monochrome in a neutral well.
- **Words:** `KARACHI VALORANT OPEN · PRESENTED BY [PARTNER]`.
- **Failure → fix:** *Logos in every corner, partner red fighting our rose* → one placement per surface; one cue system per frame.

## 8. Venue screens and physical space

- **Arc:** all of it, over hours. **Lead:** Broadcast scaled up (Daylight for bright halls and posters). **Archetype:** Pulse between matches, face-off before each, verdict after.
- **Hero:** the next match and its time, readable at 10 metres (minimum 72 px at 1080p for the hero, 32 px for supporting).
- **Dials:** Quiet up (screens run for hours); scale up.
- **Moves:** enormous hero number; scoreboard schedule.
- **Words:** `NEXT ON STATION 4 · 20:30` / "Zero Ping vs Delta Squad".
- **Failure → fix:** *Constant motion and rotating slides* → a stable layout that changes only when the state changes.

## 9. Money: payouts, receipts, fees

- **Arc:** outcome / trouble. **Lead:** Daylight (documents), Console (review screens). **Archetype:** Scoreboard / verdict.
- **Hero:** the amount and its date.
- **Dials:** Serious up, Technical up.
- **Words:** "PKR 50,000 paid to Night Owls on 18 Nov 2026, 14:20 PKT. Ref ESP-PAY-8841."
- **Failure → fix:** *Celebratory tone and rounded amounts on a financial record* → exact figures, reference numbers, no emoji or exclamations.

## 10. Bad news: delay, cancellation, rule decision, ban

- **Arc:** trouble. **Lead:** the surface's own direction, **stripped** (type only). **Archetype:** Stage, stripped: statement → reason → next step.
- **Hero:** the new fact.
- **Dials:** Quiet fully down, Serious fully up.
- **Words:** "The final now starts at 9:00 PM, one hour late, because of a server outage. Check-in stays open until 8:45 PM. Sorry for the wait." · Ban: "Your team is removed from this cup for a rules breach (section 4.2, unverified roster). You can appeal until 20 Nov, 23:59 PKT."
- **Failure → fix:** *Euphemism and brand flourishes* → plain facts, one apology, what happens next, how to appeal.

## 11. Silence: empty, waiting and idle states

- **Arc:** before anything happened. **Lead:** the surface's own. **Archetype:** Stage, small.
- **Hero:** why it's empty + the one action.
- **Words:** "No matches yet. The bracket appears here when check-in closes." · "No teams yet. Share the link or invite teams directly." [Invite teams]
- **Failure → fix:** *Sad mascot illustrations or a blank screen that looks broken* → one sentence of reason, one action.

## 12. Editorial recap or story

- **Arc:** belonging. **Lead:** Editorial. **Archetype:** Stage opener, spotlight, inline scoreboard.
- **Hero:** the moment that defined the event.
- **Words:** "The four maps that made Night Owls champions."
- **Failure → fix:** *A dashboard of stats pretending to be a story* → one column, real photos, stats inline where the story needs them.

## 13. Themed, charity or league event

- **Lead:** Themed event (L3). Build a small sub-brand world from the event's truth; keep invariants; lock up with Esportra. See `directions/themed-event.md`.
- **Failure → fix:** *A theme borrowed from a trend (neon synthwave for a charity cup)* → ask what the event is about for its people; derive the world from that.

---

## When the brief asks for something else entirely

The recipe still applies - moment, truth, idea, hero - but the direction changes. Keep the invariants; let the variables move. **Ask what the occasion needs before assuming it needs our default at full volume.**
