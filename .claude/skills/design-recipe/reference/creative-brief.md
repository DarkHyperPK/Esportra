# Creative brief and hand-off contract

A junior agent is only as good as its brief. A thin brief produces the default look; a sharp brief produces work that feels inevitable. The rule runs both ways: whoever receives a thin brief must ask (per `discovery-first`), not guess.

---

## The brief template

**A brief with empty fields is a question list, not a brief.** Send the questions back.

```
CREATIVE BRIEF - [piece name]                    Project: PROJ-XXX   Owner: [agent]

MOMENT        Where on the arc (anticipation / nerves / match / outcome / belonging / trouble)
AUDIENCE      Who exactly, in one line (role, place, experience level, device)
TRUTH         What they feel or fear right now, in their words
IDEA          One sentence, no "and"
HERO          The one element that carries the idea (number / name / face / date / state)
SIGNALS       S1 surface · S2 mode · S3 arc · S4 audience · S5 density · S6 emotion · S7 latitude · S8 constraints
DIRECTION     Proposed lead direction (+ blend) - or "open: propose 2-3 routes"
ARCHETYPE     Stage / face-off / scoreboard / path / spotlight / fork / verdict / pulse
MANDATORIES   Logos, legal lines, partners, dates, prices, exact names and spellings
FORMATS       Sizes and media (16:9 / 4:5 / 9:16 / product / push / email / print)
WORDS         Headline, caption, action drafts - or "propose three"
AVOID         Specific traps for this piece
SUCCESS       What the audience does or feels afterwards; how we will know
DELIVERABLES  Files, variants, states, deadline
ANSWERS       Links to recorded clarifications that shaped this brief
```

## A filled brief (example)

```
CREATIVE BRIEF - Karachi Valorant Open announcement       Project: PROJ-041   Owner: creative-lead

MOMENT        Anticipation - registration opens today
AUDIENCE      Grassroots Valorant five-stacks in Karachi; mostly 17-24; see it on Instagram and Discord on phones
TRUTH         "Local cups start late and nobody takes them seriously"
IDEA          This one runs like a final.
HERO          The start time: Saturday 8:00 PM PKT
SIGNALS       S1 social+Discord · S2 persuade · S3 anticipation · S4 players · S5 a handful · S6 anticipation · S7 L1 · S8 no partner, crests n/a
DIRECTION     Broadcast lead (Cinematic rejected: not a platform launch; Community rejected: audience is fluent, not new)
ARCHETYPE     Stage + scoreboard facts band
MANDATORIES   "Karachi Valorant Open", 32 teams, PKR 2,500 entry, PKR 150,000 prize, venue "Arena 9, DHA"
FORMATS       4:5 feed, 9:16 story, Discord embed, venue poster (Daylight)
WORDS         Headline "Starts at 8:00 PM. Actually." · caption SAT 14 NOV · KARACHI · action "Enter your team"
AVOID         "Limited slots!", superlatives, neon, red
SUCCESS       32 teams registered within 5 days; fewer "what time?" questions in Discord
DELIVERABLES  4 formats, 2 headline alternatives, by Thu 18:00 PKT
ANSWERS       clarifications.md #3 (hero = time, not prize), #4 (venue confirmed)
```

## The hand-off contract

What the maker returns, in this order:

1. **Understanding** - the brief restated in their own words, plus every assumption made.
2. **Open questions** - each BLOCKING / SHAPING, with the default used if unanswered.
3. **Two or three routes** - each a different honest answer (different direction, archetype or hero; never colour variants), each with a one-line rationale tied to the truth.
4. **Recommendation** - one route, and why, referencing the signals.
5. **Direction Contract** - for the recommended route (template in `direction-engine.md`).
6. **Rubric** - scored for the recommended route, with evidence per line.
7. **Final files** - every format and state requested, only after a route is chosen.

## The review loop

- Feedback names the rubric question or contract line (`critique-protocol.md`).
- A route failing Q1-4 goes back to the brief, not to polish.
- Two rounds of revision on the same point means the brief was unclear: fix the brief.

## The recipe in one breath

> Find the moment and the truth. Say one idea in one sentence. Give it one hero. Read the room and choose the direction that fits - not the one you used last time. Choose the shape of attention and dress it with our ingredients: honest words, one cue of light, two voices of type, the moves that make it ours. Taste it with the rubric. Remove one more thing. Ship it, proud but never loud.
