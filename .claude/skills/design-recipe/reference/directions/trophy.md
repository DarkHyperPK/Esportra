# Direction dossier: Trophy

> The single peak. The champions take the stage; the brand turns its volume up - for them, not for itself.

Trophy is for the top of the arc: a final's result, a season's champion, awards, MVP reveals, a team's shareable season card. It is the only direction where team colours may take the stage and where the brand allows one notch more drama. It still keeps dignity for the other side.

---

## 1. Signal profile

| Signal | Fits when | Doesn't fit when |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Surface | Result cards, champion screens, award posts, venue screens at the final, season cards | Everyday results in a table |
| S2 Mode | Experience, Persuade (share) | Operate |
| S3 Arc | Outcome at its peak | Anything else |
| S6 Emotion | Pride, celebration | Calm certainty |
| S7 Latitude | L2 | L0 |

---

## 2. Ingredients

### Ground and light
Stage black with a single warm key light on the champions (photography) or a soft warm falloff behind the crest. **The champion team's colours may take the stage for this moment** - a rule in their colour, their crest at hero scale, a subtle team-colour glow behind the crest. Rose becomes the underline under the champion's name.

### Type
Champion name Poppins 900 at maximum scale (120-200 px desktop, 64-80 px phone). Caption `CHAMPIONS · KARACHI VALORANT OPEN · 2026`. Score as a resolved versus lockup (winner white, other side grey).

### Shape
The one 45° notch on the champion card. Crest in a large square well.

### Imagery
The real team lifting the real trophy; if unavailable, the crest alone at hero scale.

### Motion
Beat of silence (600-800 ms) → one reveal (name wipes in, 500 ms) → long hold → slow push-in on the photo. In venue/stream: cut the music before the reveal; the cue sound on the reveal.

### Voice
Proud, specific, respectful: "Night Owls are champions. 3-1 over Crimson Five in a final that went the distance."

---

## 3. Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Name the runners-up with respect | Red for the loser, "rekt", skulls |
| One reveal, long hold | Confetti storms, fireworks loops |
| Team colours on the champion moment | Team colours bleeding into our product chrome afterwards |
| A shareable 4:5 and 9:16 card | Only a 16:9 that crops badly on social |

---

## 4. Worked example: champion card (4:5)

Caption top-left → crest at 30% of the frame (team colour glow behind) → champion name at hero scale → rose underline → versus lockup resolved "3-1" with the runners-up in grey → mono footer `GRAND FINAL · 18 NOV 2026 · KARACHI`. Notch on the top-right corner.
