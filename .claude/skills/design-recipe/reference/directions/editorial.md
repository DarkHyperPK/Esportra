# Direction dossier: Editorial

> A magazine for the scene. Reading first, pictures that tell the story, type that invites you to stay.

Editorial is for surfaces whose job is understanding or storytelling: match and season recaps, player features, guides, the academy, help articles, changelogs, organizer case studies, long announcements. The enemy here is the dashboard reflex - chopping a story into cards, stats and chips. A story is read top to bottom; design for the reading.

---

## 1. Signal profile

| Signal | Fits when | Doesn't fit when |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Surface | Articles, recaps, guides, help centre, academy, changelog, long social posts turned into pages | Task surfaces |
| S2 Mode | Read | Operate |
| S3 Arc | Belonging (after), anticipation (previews, explainers) | Nerves, live match |
| S4 Audience | Anyone with a few minutes: fans, players, organizers learning | Staff mid-task |
| S5 Density | Long-form, sequential | Tables, queues |
| S6 Emotion | Understanding, pride, belonging | Urgency |
| S7 Latitude | L1 | - |

---

## 2. Ingredients

### Ground
Dark stage by default, or Daylight for help/guides that are printed or read in daylight. More air than any other direction.

### Type (reading scale)
| Step | Spec |
|---|---|
| Headline | Poppins 800-900, 40-64 px desktop / 32-40 px mobile, tracking -2%, max 2-3 lines |
| Standfirst | Inter 400, 20-22 px, leading 1.45, `zinc-300` |
| Body | Inter 400, 17-19 px, leading 1.65, `zinc-200`, measure 60-70 characters |
| Subheads | Poppins 700, 22-24 px, generous space above (48 px) |
| Pull quote | Poppins 800, 28-32 px, with a 2 px rose rule at the left - the page's single cue |
| Dateline / credits / captions | Mono 11 px caps, `zinc-500`: `KARACHI · 18 NOV 2026 · 6 MIN READ` |
| Inline stats | A small scoreboard grid set into the column, never a dashboard |

### Layout
One strong column (max 680 px) centred or offset left; images may break the column (full-bleed or 1.3× column) **once per section**; captions under images in mono. Table of contents sticky on wide screens for guides.

### Imagery
Documentary photography of the real scene is the star: wide establishing shots, then close details (hands, keycaps, faces lit by screens). Every image captioned with who, where, when.

### Motion
Almost none. No scroll-triggered reveals on body text. At most a gentle arrive on images (opacity only, 240 ms).

### Voice
The caster at length: specific, warm, factual, quoting players in their own words. Headlines state the story ("The four maps that made Night Owls champions"), not clickbait.

---

## 3. Signature moves

Tight display + wide caption (headline + dateline) · inline scoreboard grid · lower-third for quoted players · cue light as the pull-quote rule.

---

## 4. Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| One column, generous measure | Cards and chips chopping the story |
| Real photos with captions | Stock imagery or decorative illustration |
| Stats inline where the story needs them | A stats dashboard at the top |
| Guides with a sticky contents list | Accordion walls hiding the answer |

---

## 5. Worked example: season recap

Signals: S1 article · S2 read · S3 belonging · S4 fans/players · S5 long-form · S6 pride · S7 L1.

Structure: dateline caption → headline "Night Owls, from a cafe cup to the Karachi final" → standfirst → full-bleed photo (the lift) → body with two subheads → inline scoreboard (the four maps) → pull quote from the IGL with rose rule → closing photo → "Enter the next cup" as a quiet text action at the end, not a banner at the top.
