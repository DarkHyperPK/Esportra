# Direction dossier: Co-brand

> A shared stage. Agree who leads before designing anything.

Co-brand applies whenever a publisher, sponsor, venue chain or league brings its own brand system: presented-by tournaments, publisher-sanctioned events, sponsor activations, partner landing pages.

---

## 1. First question: the relationship

Ask (per `discovery-first`) before any layout. Everything follows from the answer.

| Relationship | Who leads the look | Lockup |
|---|---|---|
| **Presented by** (we lead) | Esportra direction (usually Broadcast/Cinematic); partner mark as a signature | `KARACHI VALORANT OPEN · PRESENTED BY [PARTNER]` in mono; partner mark in a neutral well |
| **In partnership with** (equal) | A neutral shared frame: our stage + their accent as a second, *separated* voice | Side-by-side lockup with a hairline divider, equal visual weight |
| **Powered by Esportra** (partner leads) | Partner's system (their palette, type, rules) | Small "Powered by Esportra" mark in their footer or bracket module |

---

## 2. Rules

- **One cue light per frame, owned by the leading brand.** Two accent systems in one frame fight; the viewer can't tell what matters.
- **Partner marks are never recoloured, stretched or cropped.** Use the partner's approved variants (monochrome where supplied).
- **Clear space** around every mark: at least the height of the mark's cap letter.
- **Our invariants hold regardless of who leads:** honest words, player-first, accessibility, exact time and money.
- **The event stays the hero**, never the sponsor, unless the partner leads.
- Get partner guidelines as files (S8) before designing; store them in the project's `creative/` folder.

---

## 3. Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Decide the relationship first | Design first and "add the logo" later |
| Monochrome partner mark on our stage | Full-colour logos in every corner |
| Separate accent systems by region | Rose and a partner's red in one frame |

---

## 4. Worked example: presented-by tournament page

Broadcast lead. Header caption `KARACHI VALORANT OPEN · PRESENTED BY [PARTNER]`; partner mark monochrome in a 64 px dark well at the header's right; the event's date as hero; partner appears once more in the prize scoreboard ("Prize pool supported by [Partner]"). No partner colour anywhere else.
