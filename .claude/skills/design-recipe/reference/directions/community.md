# Direction dossier: Community

> People first. Warm, welcoming, human - without becoming cute.

Community is for surfaces where belonging and reassurance matter more than speed: first-run onboarding, player and team profiles, community highlights, grassroots cups, charity events, parent-facing explanations. The audience may be new, unsure or simply there for the people. The design should feel like a friendly venue owner showing you around - not a control room, and not a toy shop.

---

## 1. Signal profile

| Signal | Fits when | Doesn't fit when |
|---|---|---|
| S1 Surface | Onboarding, profiles, team pages, community posts, charity/grassroots event pages | Live operations, payments review |
| S2 Mode | Operate (light tasks) · Persuade (belonging) · Read (short) | Heavy Operate |
| S3 Arc | Anticipation (for newcomers), belonging | Live match, trouble |
| S4 Audience | Newcomers, parents, casual players, community members | Staff mid-task |
| S5 Density | One to a handful | Tables |
| S6 Emotion | Belonging, reassurance, warmth | Urgency, certainty-at-speed |
| S7 Latitude | L1 | - |

---

## 2. Ingredients

### Ground and light
Broadcast ground warmed slightly: greys shift toward `stone`/`neutral` (`#A8A29E`, `#78716C`), surfaces `#141212`-`#1C1917`; or Daylight for parent-facing pages. **Team colours get more room** - inside their crests, on jersey photos, as a thin team-colour rule on a team page - but never on our chrome. Rose remains the single cue.

### Type
| Step | Spec |
|---|---|
| Headline | Poppins 800, 32-44 px, sentence case, friendlier (no tight tracking; 0 to -1%) |
| Body | Inter 400, 16-17 px, leading 1.6 |
| Captions | Fewer mono captions; when used, `zinc-400` for readability |
| Names | Inter 600, 16-18 px; handles in Poppins 800 |

### Shape and space
People shapes lead: round avatars at 48-96 px, prominent. Cards may use a subtle 2-4 px radius only for people/profile cards (a deliberate exception - document it in the contract); structure stays square. Spacing ladder at ~1.25×. Fewer hairlines, more air.

### Imagery
Groups, faces, candid moments, hands, venues full of people. Real community photos, with consent. Crests large and proud on team pages.

### Motion
Gentle arrivals (220-260 ms, softer easing `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)`). One small welcome moment is allowed on first run (e.g. the player's name settling in).

### Voice
The caster, warm and plain. "Welcome, Ayesha. Let's get your team into its first cup." Never cute: no mascots, no exclamation marks in product copy, no emoji in product.

---

## 3. Signature moves

Lower-third (for people) · the fork (choices on first run) · the path (with only step one lit) · cue light (the next step).

---

## 4. Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Faces and names at the centre | Anonymous grids of stats |
| One clear next step | A tour of everything |
| Team colours inside their frames | Team colours tinting the UI |
| Warm, plain words | Mascots, jokes, emoji, "Hey gamer!" |

---

## 5. Worked example: first-run welcome (new player, phone)

Signals: S1 product · S2 operate (light) · S3 anticipation · S4 newcomer · S5 handful · S6 belonging · S7 L1.

Stage (small): "Welcome, Ayesha." → Fork: two cards, "Join a cup this weekend" (badge: Most popular) / "Create a team with friends" → Path below with step 1 lit: "Pick a game → Join or create a team → Check in on the day" → one white action. A photo strip of real local cups at the bottom as social proof.
