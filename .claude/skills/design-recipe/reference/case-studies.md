# Case studies: the recipe end to end

Three worked cases showing the whole method: questions, answers, truth, idea, hero, signals, direction choice (and the directions rejected), contract, composition, words and tasting. They are different on purpose: a product surface, a campaign and a bad-news message. Notice that the same brand produces three very different pieces.

---

## Case 1 - Product surface: captain's check-in (phone)

**Objective (from the CEO):** "Captains keep missing check-in. Fix it."

### Understanding and questions
The Creative Lead explored the current flow: check-in is a small button inside the tournament page, visible only after scrolling; reminders don't exist. Questions asked (with defaults):

1. **[BLOCKING]** Where do captains usually are at check-in time? A) On their phone, often in a Discord call (Recommended) B) At a desk. → *Answer: A.*
2. **[BLOCKING]** Must every player check in, or only the captain for the team? A) Captain checks in the team (Recommended - matches current rules) B) Each player. → *Answer: A, but show who's online.*
3. **[SHAPING]** Push, email or both for reminders? Default: push at T-30 and T-10, email at T-30. → *Confirmed.*

### Truth, idea, hero
- Truth: "I didn't know it had opened, and when I found the button it was already closed."
- Idea: *You'll know exactly when, and it takes one tap.*
- Hero: the time left.

### Signals and direction
S1 product (phone) · S2 operate · S3 nerves · S4 captain · S5 one fact + a short list · S6 reassurance · S7 L0 · S8 none.
- **Chosen: Broadcast** - fluent audience under pressure; the scorebug's clarity.
- Rejected: Community (warmth slows the one decision); Command Console (captain is not an expert operator; density wrong).

### Contract (excerpt)
THESIS "You'll know exactly when, and it takes one tap." · ARCHETYPE Stage (small) + scoreboard list · HERO minutes left at 72 px · MOVES caption over hero number, cue light (2 px under the timer), scoreboard grid · WILL NOT: countdown animations, red urgency, multiple buttons.

### Composition and words
Caption `KARACHI VALORANT OPEN · CHECK-IN` → "12:40" + "minutes left" → "Night Owls · 3 of 5 online" → roster grid with pills → sticky primary "Check in your team". After check-in: verdict state "You're checked in. First match 8:30 PM, Station 4."

### Tasting
Q1-4: 2 each. Q5: 2 (rose only under the timer). Q12: push copy "Check-in is open. 30 minutes to check in Night Owls." Total 25/26. Removed: a "Rules" link moved below the fold.

## Case 2 - Campaign: launching tournament hosting to organizers

**Objective:** "Launch hosting to community organizers in Karachi and Lahore."

### Questions
1. **[BLOCKING]** Who is the core audience? A) Cafe/LAN owners and Discord community admins running small cups (Recommended) B) Established leagues. → *A.*
2. **[BLOCKING]** Which proof can we use? A) Real results from the beta (3 organizers, 41 cups) (Recommended) B) No numbers. → *A, with permission from two organizers.*
3. **[SHAPING]** Formats: landing hero, 4:5 feed, 9:16 story, Discord banner. → *Confirmed + a venue poster.*

### Truth, idea, hero
- Truth: "Local cups start late and end in a Discord argument."
- Idea: *Run your cup like a final.*
- Hero: the word "final."

### Signals and direction
Landing/social · persuade · anticipation · organizers · one fact · ambition · **L2**.
- **Chosen: Cinematic lead + Broadcast facts band.**
- Routes presented to the CEO: A) Cinematic hero word "final." over an empty venue (recommended); B) Face-off: "Your cup" vs "a final", side by side; C) Spotlight: a real organizer, lower-third quote. CEO chose A, asked to reuse C as the second band.

### Composition and words
Hero: "Run your cup like a final." at 180 px, caption `FOR ORGANIZERS · FREE TO START`, action "Host a cup". Band 2 (scoreboard): "Brackets · Check-in · Disputes · Payouts". Band 3 (spotlight): real organizer quote. Band 4 (fork): Quick cup / Full event. Story format: "final." + action only. Poster (Daylight for the venue wall): the same line in ink with a QR code.

### Tasting
25/26 on the landing; the story scored 1 on Q12 until the supporting line was cut.

## Case 3 - Bad news: a delayed final

**Situation:** A server outage delays the Karachi final by an hour; 400 people are waiting.

### Questions (fast, one round)
1. **[BLOCKING]** New start time confirmed? → *9:00 PM PKT.*
2. **[BLOCKING]** Does check-in stay open? → *Until 8:45 PM.*
3. **[SHAPING]** Channels: in-app banner, push, Discord, venue screen. → *Confirmed.*

### Direction
The surface's own direction, **stripped**: type only, no imagery, no flourishes, no rose except on the new time. Dials: Quiet fully down, Serious fully up.

### Words
In-app / push: "The final now starts at 9:00 PM, one hour late, because of a server outage. Check-in stays open until 8:45 PM. Sorry for the wait." Venue screen: `GRAND FINAL · NEW START` / "9:00 PM" / "Server outage. Check-in open until 8:45 PM."

### What we avoided
"A slight adjustment to the schedule", playful copy, blaming the host provider by name, hiding the reason.

### Tasting
The rubric's Q8 (signature) scored 1 by design (type only); approved with a note: bad news trades signature for plainness.

---

## What the cases teach

- The same brand produced a quiet phone view, a loud campaign and a plain notice - because the **signals** differed.
- Every case started with **questions**, and the answers changed the work (who the audience is, what proof exists, what the facts are).
- Every direction choice named what it **rejected** and why.
- Every piece was **tasted** and something was **removed**.
