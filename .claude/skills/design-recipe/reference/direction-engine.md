# The direction engine

How to choose the right dish for the table, every time, instead of serving the house special to everyone.

A **direction** is a coherent visual world: ground, light, type, shape, density, imagery, motion energy and voice, chosen together because they serve one kind of moment. Directions are not skins. Swapping the accent colour on the same layout is not a new direction.

The brand's **invariants** (see `esportra-brand` section 3) hold in every direction. The **variables** move.

---

## Step 1 - Read the room: the eight signals

Score each signal for the piece in front of you. Write the scores down; they become part of the Direction Contract and make the choice defensible.

| # | Signal | Values | What it pushes |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | **Surface & medium** | Product screen · landing page · social feed · story/vertical · video · venue/big screen · email · print/document · deck | Scale, density, how much motion is possible, light vs dark ground |
| S2 | **Mode** (impeccable's taxonomy) | Persuade (decide & act) · Operate (complete a task) · Read (understand) · Experience (be inside the work) | Expression vs restraint; how much brand vs how much utility |
| S3 | **Moment on the arc** | Anticipation · nerves · match · outcome · belonging · trouble | Volume: loud before, silent during, proud after, plain in trouble |
| S4 | **Audience & literacy** | Pros, staff (expert, daily use) · captains/players (fluent, mobile) · newcomers/parents · partners/press | Density, jargon, how much teaching the design does |
| S5 | **Information density** | One fact · a handful · a table · hundreds of rows | Archetype and layout system |
| S6 | **Emotional target** | Calm/trust · anticipation · pride/celebration · belonging/warmth · urgency (real) · reassurance | Temperature, motion energy, imagery |
| S7 | **Brand latitude** | L0 strict · L1 flexible · L2 expressive · L3 shared/sub-brand | How far variables may move |
| S8 | **Hard constraints** | Partner guidelines · legal lines · assets available (photos? only type?) · accessibility needs · device/perf limits · languages | Rules out directions |

### Brand latitude levels

| Level | Where | What may move |
|---|---|---|
| **L0 - Strict** | Core product UI, transactional flows, payments, rules, disputes | Only density and emphasis. Tokens, type, shape and voice are fixed. |
| **L1 - Flexible** | Owned marketing, emails, help, profiles, venue screens | Ground (dark/light), imagery, layout drama, motion energy. |
| **L2 - Expressive** | Campaigns, launches, trophy moments, event key art | Type scale extremes, grid breaks, art direction, one extra accent moment, stronger motion. |
| **L3 - Shared / sub-brand** | Co-brands, league or charity identities, themed events | Palette, typeface pairing, texture and motif may belong to the event or partner; Esportra invariants and a clear "powered by" relationship remain. |

## Step 2 - The direction library

Each direction lists when to choose it, its ingredients, its natural archetypes and signature moves, and its guardrails. Use the signals to shortlist two or three, then pick.

### 1. Broadcast (the default world)

- **Choose when:** S3 is match/outcome/anticipation, S2 is Operate or Persuade on core surfaces, S7 is L0-L1. The house style; the brand at rest.
- **Ground & light:** stage black, charcoal surfaces, bone white type, one rose cue.
- **Type:** Poppins black display (tight) over wide mono captions; Inter for reading.
- **Shape & space:** square cuts, hairlines, scoreboard grid, doubling space ladder.
- **Imagery:** team crests in dark wells; game art as a window; real players lit by screens.
- **Motion:** broadcast wipe; stillness at rest; one reveal.
- **Archetypes:** face-off, scoreboard, pulse, verdict, stage.
- **Guardrails:** the easiest direction to apply lazily. Justify it from the signals, don't assume it.

### 2. Command Console

- **Choose when:** S4 is expert daily users (organizers, staff, admins, moderators), S5 is a table or hundreds of rows, S2 is Operate, S3 is often "match" (live operations).
- **Ground & light:** the Broadcast ground, but quieter still: almost no accent, semantic tones do the work (pending, approved, disputed).
- **Type:** smaller scale steps (display rarely used), tabular numbers everywhere, mono for IDs and times.
- **Shape & space:** tighter ladder (0.75×), sticky headers and action bars, keyboard-reachable everything, dense rows with generous row hover.
- **Imagery:** almost none; crests at 20-24 px.
- **Motion:** feedback only (confirm, alert). Zero decorative motion.
- **Archetypes:** scoreboard, pulse, fork (for bulk decisions).
- **Guardrails:** density must be *organised*, never cramped: group by intent, cut by permission, put "needs you" first. Test at 1440 px and at 390 px.

### 3. Editorial

- **Choose when:** S2 is Read, S5 is long-form, S6 is understanding or belonging. Recaps, player stories, guides, the academy, changelogs, long social captions turned into posts.
- **Ground & light:** dark or daylight (L1), larger text, generous measure (60-70 ch), more air.
- **Type:** display used as headlines and pull quotes; body at 17-19 px; captions as datelines and credits.
- **Shape & space:** a strong column, images breaking the column occasionally (one per section), footnote-style captions.
- **Imagery:** documentary photography of the real scene is the star.
- **Motion:** almost none; reading is still.
- **Archetypes:** stage (article opener), spotlight, scoreboard (inline stats), path (journeys).
- **Guardrails:** no dashboard chrome on reading surfaces; no cards chopping a story into tiles.

### 4. Cinematic

- **Choose when:** S2 is Persuade, S3 is anticipation, S7 is L2, S1 is landing, key art, trailer or campaign hero.
- **Ground & light:** deep black with a single light source (a stage spot, a monitor glow); one soft falloff allowed.
- **Type:** extreme scale (hero word 6-10× the caption), one grid break, a single line of copy that lands.
- **Shape & space:** huge rest areas; the hero pushed off-centre; the action released bottom-right.
- **Imagery:** full-bleed real photography or type alone - never stock, never a collage.
- **Motion:** held stillness, one decisive reveal, a slow push-in; sound design cut before the reveal.
- **Archetypes:** stage, face-off, spotlight.
- **Guardrails:** one idea only; features go one scroll down. Must still pass the three-second test on a phone.

### 5. Community

- **Choose when:** S4 includes newcomers, parents or grassroots players; S6 is belonging or reassurance; S3 is anticipation or belonging; onboarding, profiles, team pages, community highlights, charity cups.
- **Ground & light:** Broadcast ground warmed slightly (warmer greys) or daylight for L1; more room for team colour inside frames.
- **Type:** larger body text, friendlier sentence-case headlines, fewer mono captions.
- **Shape & space:** people shapes lead (round avatars prominent), more generous spacing, fewer hairlines.
- **Imagery:** groups, faces, venues, hands; candid over posed.
- **Motion:** gentle arrivals; a small welcome moment is allowed.
- **Archetypes:** fork, path, spotlight.
- **Guardrails:** warm is not cute - no mascots, no exclamation marks, no emoji in product copy. Still one hero.

### 6. Daylight

- **Choose when:** S1 is print, documents, receipts, invoices, payout statements, email, or a physical venue in daylight; S6 is trust or reassurance; S2 is Read or Operate around money or rules.
- **Ground & light:** the stage with the lights up: bone paper, black ink, greys for context, rose still the single cue.
- **Type:** same families; heavier weight contrast to survive print and bright screens.
- **Shape & space:** tables and hairlines in ink; the scoreboard grid on paper.
- **Imagery:** minimal; logos and crests in monochrome where partners allow.
- **Motion:** none (or email-safe only).
- **Archetypes:** scoreboard, verdict, path.
- **Guardrails:** money and rules must be exact, dated and time-zoned; never decorative.

### 7. Trophy

- **Choose when:** S3 is outcome at its peak (a final, a season, an award), S6 is pride, S7 is L2. The one time the brand turns its volume up.
- **Ground & light:** stage black with a single warm key on the champions; the **champion's team colours may take the stage** for this moment only.
- **Type:** the champion's name at maximum scale; the score as a resolved versus lockup.
- **Shape & space:** the one 45-degree notch; a crest at hero scale; stillness around it.
- **Imagery:** the real team, the real trophy, the real moment.
- **Motion:** beat of silence, one reveal, long hold, slow push-in.
- **Archetypes:** verdict, spotlight.
- **Guardrails:** celebrate the winner, respect the runner-up (grey, never red, named with dignity). No confetti storms, no "rekt".

### 8. Co-brand

- **Choose when:** S8 includes partner or publisher guidelines; S7 is L3.
- **Rules:** agree the relationship first - *presented by* (we lead), *in partnership with* (equal), *powered by Esportra* (partner leads). The leading system sets ground, type and palette; the other appears as a clean lockup. Our invariants (honest words, player-first, accessibility) always hold.
- **Guardrails:** never let two accent systems fight in one frame; one cue light, owned by whichever brand leads.

### 9. Themed event

- **Choose when:** an event has its own identity (a seasonal cup, a league, a charity partnership, a retro night, a women's circuit) and S7 is L3.
- **Rules:** design a small sub-brand world (palette, typeface pairing, motif) that fits the event's truth, framed by Esportra invariants and a consistent "on Esportra" lockup. Product surfaces keep the core system; only event-owned surfaces (key art, social, venue) wear the theme.
- **Guardrails:** the theme must come from the event's real subject (its city, cause, era, community), not from a trend.

### Mixing

Directions can blend on one surface when signals conflict, but one leads: *Broadcast led, Editorial body* (a match recap), *Console led, Broadcast header* (a live operations screen), *Community led, Daylight ground* (a parent-facing guide). State the lead and the blend in the contract.

## Step 3 - Signals to directions: quick map

| Signals | Lead direction | Common blend |
|---|---|---|
| Operate + expert + many rows | Command Console | Broadcast header |
| Operate + players + mobile + nerves | Broadcast | Community copy warmth |
| Persuade + anticipation + L2 | Cinematic | Broadcast facts band |
| Read + long-form | Editorial | Broadcast stats inline |
| Belonging + newcomers | Community | Daylight ground |
| Outcome peak + L2 | Trophy | Broadcast facts |
| Money, rules, print, email | Daylight | Console tables |
| Partner guidelines present | Co-brand | Whichever system leads |
| Event with its own identity | Themed event | Broadcast in product |
| Trouble (any surface) | The surface's direction, **stripped**: type only, no flourishes | - |

If two directions score equally, that is a question for the CEO (per `discovery-first`), not a coin toss. Present both as routes.

## Step 4 - Routes that differ in kind

When a brief leaves direction open, produce two or three **routes**. Each route must differ from the others in at least one of: direction, archetype, hero. For each route write a **direction card**:

```
ROUTE A - "[name the idea, not the style]"
Direction      Cinematic (lead) + Broadcast facts band
Archetype      Stage
Hero           The start time, 8:00 PM, at 40% of the frame
Why            The truth is "local cups start late"; the time *is* the promise
Signals        S1 landing · S2 persuade · S3 anticipation · S7 L2
Risk           Less room for format and prize details above the fold
```

Recommend one route and say why, referencing the signals and the truth.

## Step 5 - The Direction Contract

Once chosen (and approved, when the brief was open), write it into the project folder (`.claude/company/projects/PROJ-XXX/creative/direction-contract.md`) or the task hand-off. Every downstream agent builds against it; reviewers judge against it.

```markdown
# Direction Contract - [piece]

THESIS        One sentence: the idea.
TRUTH         The audience truth it answers.
AUDIENCE      Who, and their moment on the arc.
SIGNALS       S1..S8 scores.
DIRECTION     Lead direction (+ blend), and latitude level.
ARCHETYPE     Per band / per screen.
HERO          The one element; its scale relationship to everything else.
INGREDIENTS   Ground · light/accent · type · shape · density · imagery · motion energy.
MOVES         The 2+ signature moves used, and which are deliberately not used.
WORDS         Headline, caption, action (or the voice notes for product copy).
FORMATS       Every size and medium, with what changes in each.
WILL NOT      The specific things this piece will not do (from anti-patterns and the brief).
SUCCESS       What the audience does or feels afterwards.
```

A contract that could be pasted onto a different brief unchanged is not a contract. Rewrite it.
