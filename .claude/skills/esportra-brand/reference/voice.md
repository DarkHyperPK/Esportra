# Esportra voice

Words are the ingredient people remember longest. A layout is seen; a sentence is repeated in group chats.

## Doctrine

1. **Say the thing.** Lead with the fact the reader needs.
2. **Be specific.** Numbers, names, times, places. Specific is credible; vague is marketing.
3. **Talk to one person.** "You", "your team". Never "users", "participants", "gamers".
4. **Players are the subject.** "Night Owls won the final", not "Esportra crowns...".
5. **Calm is confident.** No exclamation marks in product or service copy; at most one in a campaign, at the peak of a real celebration.
6. **Short, then shorter.** Write it, then remove a third.
7. **Never fake it.** No invented urgency, inflated numbers or unprovable claims.

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Anticipation | Inviting, confident, a little proud | "Karachi's best five-stacks. One weekend. Starts 8:00 PM." |
| Nerves | Instructional, exact, reassuring | "You're checked in. Your first match is at 8:30 PM on Station 4." |
| The match | Almost silent; state only | "Map 3 · 9-6" |
| Outcome | Respectful, warm, specific | "Crimson Five pushed the champions to four maps." |
| Belonging | Human, generous, shareable | "Your team's season, in one card." |
| Trouble | Plain, accountable, forward-looking | "The final now starts at 9:00 PM, one hour late, because of a server outage. Check-in stays open until 8:45 PM." |

## Lexicon

| We say | We don't say |
|---|---|
| Enter your team | Sign up now!! |
| Players, teams, hosts | Users, participants, gamers |
| Starts 8:00 PM PKT | Starting soon |
| Champions / runners-up | Winners / losers |
| Couldn't save. Try again. | Oops! Something went wrong :( |
| Official, fair, on time | Epic, insane, next-level, elite, ultimate |
| PKR 150,000 | Huge cash prizes |
| This one runs like a final | The #1 esports platform |

## Headline patterns

- **The fact as headline:** "Starts at 8:00 PM. Actually."
- **The understatement:** "A final worth the drive."
- **The name as headline:** "Night Owls." + caption `CHAMPIONS · KARACHI VALORANT OPEN`
- **The promise, plainly:** "Every match, official." (sparingly)

Avoid question headlines ("Ready to compete?"), puns on game names, and lines any brand could say.

## Captions (the scorebug voice)

Uppercase mono, wide tracking, grey. Nouns and numbers only, middots between facts, max four facts, no verbs, no end punctuation. Never repeats the headline.

## Buttons

A verb plus the thing, two to four words: "Enter your team", "Watch the final", "Book a station". Never "Submit", "OK", "Click here". Destructive actions name the consequence and confirm: "Remove Night Owls? They lose their slot."

## Numbers, time, money

Local time with zone when shared beyond the city ("8:00 PM PKT"). Local currency in full ("PKR 150,000"). Scores with an en dash ("13–7"). Relative time only when short and live ("in 15 minutes").

## Languages, emoji, slang

Write in the audience's real language (English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Arabic...); keep game terms as players use them (IGL, clutch, ace). No emoji in product/service copy; at most one meaningful emoji on social; never skulls, crying faces or flames about real people. We understand slang and quote players using it; we don't adopt it as the brand's voice.

## Bad news

Say what happened, say sorry once, say what happens next. No euphemism ("a slight adjustment"), no blame, no playful tone, no brand flourishes.
