**What we do:**

## Partnership Tiers

We offer three partnership levels, each with increasing visibility and engagement opportunities.

### 💎 Diamond Partner
Your entry into the Esportra ecosystem.

- ✅ Brand listing on our **Partners page** with logo, description, and CTA
- ✅ Logo placement in the **Homepage Partner Ticker**
- ✅ Custom **call-to-action button** (e.g., "Shop Now", "Learn More")
- ✅ **Promo code / discount** displayed to all users
- ✅ 1 showcase image

---

### 🟢 Ascendant Partner
Deeper integration and full performance visibility.

- ✅ Everything in Diamond, plus:
- ✅ **Banner image upload** — high-impact visual on your partner section
- ✅ Up to **5 gallery images** in an animated showcase carousel
- ✅ Access to the **Partner Analytics Portal**:
  - Total impressions & unique impressions
  - Click-through rate (CTR)
  - 30-day performance trend charts
  - Audience demographics (country & age breakdowns)

---

### 🟡 Radiant Partner *(Premium)*
Maximum visibility and priority placement across the platform.

- ✅ Everything in Ascendant, plus:
- ✅ Up to **8 gallery images** with full-screen showcase
- ✅ **Highest priority ranking** — your brand appears first, everywhere
- ✅ **Homepage Hero Placement** — large interactive banner card on the landing page
- ✅ **Tournament page ad placements** — branded banners inside active tournament pages
- ✅ **Stream overlay integration** — your brand on tournament broadcasts

## Partner Portal

Every approved partner gets access to a **private dashboard** where they can:
